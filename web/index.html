Read your language on the CMF Watch 3 Pro

Adds Greek, Georgian or Armenian letters to the watch's own font, in your browser. Free and open source. Not affiliated with Nothing or CMF.

Choose your languages, add the official firmware from Nothing, connect the watch over Bluetooth and install. The firmware file never leaves your computer. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone.

Ελληνικά, γεωργιανά και αρμενικά στο CMF Watch 3 Pro, απευθείας από τον browser.
